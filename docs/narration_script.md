# Three-minute recording guide

The brief asks for **your own narration**, so this is a shot list with the points to make, not a script to read. One take, about 3:00. Before recording: open the app, go to **Imports**, click **Clear all data**, then go to Overview.

## 0:00-1:00 A useful workflow and one metric
1. **Imports → Import batch 1 (initial)**, then **Overview.** Say: "44 records from one day: 30 calls and 14 meetings."
2. Click **ⓘ** on **Usable text**. Say: "18 of 44, 40.9%. The denominator is every record in view. Twenty calls have no text, and six meetings are silent or were never captured, so most of the day cannot be judged. It is listed in intake, not hidden." (Before the update it reads 17 of 44; the update on the Imports page takes it to 18.)
3. Pick a person in the top filter, then back to Everyone. Say: "Calls belong to the rep; meetings to the organiser."
4. In **Needs attention first** open **M04 Cedarwave**. Say: "Existing customer, prepaid card programme closing. Priority 80." Click the first evidence chip and read turn 11: moving from prepaid to credit is the main issue. "Every claim jumps to the exact turn."

## 1:00-1:50 A difficult routing decision (C09)
1. **Inbox →** open **C09**. Say: "The source calls this 'referred'. The banner says the text reads as uninterested."
2. Click the evidence chips: "we aren't looking for anything outside the organisation" and "No, please don't use my name."
3. Say: "I route it to Outreach log with a coaching flag, not Deal next steps. The risk is I under-count a real lead, so it stays flagged for a person."
4. Tick **Deal next steps**, add a note, **Save manual route**. Say: "A manager can overrule me; it is stored separately." Then **Reset to suggestion**.

## 1:50-3:00 Duplicate and update test
1. **Imports.** Click **Import batch 1 again, reordered**. Say: "0 added, 0 updated, 44 ignored. Still 44."
2. Click **Set M07 route manually**. Say: "No transcript yet, so a provisional manual route."
3. Click **Import update (revision 2)**. Say: "One updated, none added. 44 records, 18 usable."
4. Click **Import batch 1 again (older revision)**. Say: "Ignored: an older revision cannot overwrite a newer one."
5. Click **Refresh the page**. Read the Live checks: M07 revision 2, analysed, Customer follow-up (manual), suggestion changed, manual kept. Say: "The new text changed the suggestion, my choice survived, and I decide whether to keep it."
6. Optional 10 seconds: **Export** page, show the manifest row for M07 (manual, current).

If you run long, drop the person filter and the C09 manual-route step.
